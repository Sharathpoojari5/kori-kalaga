Each bird gets one session a day. Training builds it up but wears down its condition, and a tired bird starts a fight weaker.

  1. RAISE

    Train and rest

    Run for stamina, spar for power, work footwork for speed. Rest and feed to get condition back.

  2. READ

    Size up the ground

    Each day new challengers arrive. Check their breed, katti, record and how they carry themselves.

  3. DECIDE

    Fight or walk away

    Pick the bird to bring. If the rival looks too strong, or your bird is worn out, you can walk away.
